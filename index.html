Keeping a Naples Home Fresh Between Visits

Even with professional help, a few simple habits keep your space looking its best. Place doormats at every entry to trap sand and grit before they scratch your floors. Run a dehumidifier or air conditioning fan in damp rooms to discourage mildew, and wipe bathroom surfaces after use to slow soap scum build-up.

Wipe kitchen counters daily, empty small trash bins often and open windows on dry mornings for fresh airflow. Little routines like these extend the results of each scheduled clean and make the next visit even more effective.

Seasonal residents can also arrange a pre-arrival clean so the home is dust-free, fresh and ready the moment you walk in, plus a post-season clean before you head north.
